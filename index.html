01 AIRPORT · BOTH WAYS

Reserve two vehicles

For nine people with bags, ask for two vans or a licensed minibus with enough luggage space. A typical “nine-seat” van holds eight passengers plus its driver. On arrival, follow the driver's meeting instructions; on Monday, leave the Guting area around midday and allow a generous airport buffer.

Reserve a Taiwanese table

THE EASY ROUTES

Across town

Use the MRT for most city stops

Guting is on the Green and Orange lines. Shandao Temple (Fu Hang), Zhongshan (Ningxia) and Songshan (Raohe) are easy rides; group taxis are useful late at night. Tap an EasyCard and follow station signs.
